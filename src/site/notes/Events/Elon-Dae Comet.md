A comet that comes by Pulchra Terra every 500 years. It is said to be the heart of a dead god, giving great power when it passes by the world; but without the [[Locations/Chain Franchises/Celestial Trees\|Celestial Trees]] it would overwhelm life as we know it, and dangerously powerful boons could be granted.

Arrives on [[,Time/Timeline 2.0#Simbarr 15th, 8310b - Sokturay - Waxing Gibbous\|Simbarr 15th, 8310b]].
