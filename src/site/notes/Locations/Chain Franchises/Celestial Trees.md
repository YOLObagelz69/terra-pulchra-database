#### The Trees

---

Truly ginormous trees that shine with beautiful golden light. Their purpose is to repel the enormous amounts of power that is expelled by the [[Events/Elon-Dae Comet\|Elon-Dae Comet]] when it returns to Pulchra Terra. Without them the comet would allow powerful boons that could threaten the natural order.

#### The Animals

---

Each tree has a Celestial Beast that acts as a conduit for the tree's power. Upon the death of an avatar, the tree and all life around it dies rapidly, with it having only been ~30 hours before the tree in the [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Oalorial/Flava Forest\|Flava Forest]] wilted and died after the poa[[Locations/Chain Franchises/Celestial Trees\|The Bird]]he Bird]]. 

Ordered to be hunted and harvested for specific parts by [[NPCs/Charlie/Charlie\|Charlie]], who seemed to be a middleman for an unknown buyer.

The Bear
  A giant golden bear that lives under the tree of [[Locations/Continent of Nagare Ni Sakaiatte Tatsue/Nagare Ni Sakaiatte Tatsue\|Nagare Ni Sakaiatte Tatsue]]. It is being hunted for its pelt.
The Bird
  In the [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Oalorial/Flava Forest\|Flava Forest]]. It was poached for its beak, causing the death of [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum's]] Tree.
The Deer
  Located in [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Fallzeer/Le Buisson D'Aphrodite\|Le Buisson D'Aphrodite]]. It is being hunted for its antlers.
The Octopus
  At the tree deep within the [[Locations/Oceans and Seas/Inanis Ocean\|Inanis Ocean]]. It is being hunted for its eyes.

A new Celestial Beast will be chosen in time by the forest, but with poachers specifically hunting for the anchors and the [[Events/Elon-Dae Comet\|Elon-Dae Comet]] arriving soon, we need to find a quicker option.


###### Anchors
Each animal has a specific anchor that makes it a Celestial Beast, anchoring (heh, get it?) all the life in the region to the beast and its designated tree.
