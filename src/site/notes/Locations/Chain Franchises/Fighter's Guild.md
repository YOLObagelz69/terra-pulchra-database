A place for fighters and the like to bunk and train for adventures to come. 
Located in most major cities.

The Guild-master by the name of [[NPCs/Carthal\|Carthal]] can gift special abilities to the player party when they do major heroic feats.
	Gave [[!Players/Mháthair Nádúr\|Mháthair Nádúr]] the ability Retribution for saving the folk of [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Honeywood\|Honeywood]]
	Cursed [[!Players/Texaqou\|Texaqou]] with the "get a life" curse.
