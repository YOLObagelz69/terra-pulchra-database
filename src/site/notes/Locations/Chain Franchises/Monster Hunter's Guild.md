A guild of exotic game hunters who hunt down large creatures who are causing issues. 
They are not government funded, so their methods are... uncouth. If you attempt to turn in a bounty but are not registered to the guild, then that is considered terms of elimination. They also do not mind if you eliminate a different bounty group for their completed bounty, they only care if the bounty is turned in. This also applies to hunters hunting you for your bounty, as well.
