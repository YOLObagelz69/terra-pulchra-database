The elven continent, which contains the nations of [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Fallzeer/Fallzeer\|Fallzeer]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Cynndarr/Cynndarr\|Cynndarr]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Cyllvarr/Cyllvarr\|Cyllvarr]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Korrmear/Korrmear\|Korrmear]]. 

Within the Elven Dynasty, they are so intolerant to the lower class that the lower class is completely nonexistent, as everyone is considered equal enough. They are still completely intolerant to other races cultures, ideals and general status.
