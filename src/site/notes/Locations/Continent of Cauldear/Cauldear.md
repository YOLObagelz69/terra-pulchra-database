The elven continent, which contains the regions of [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Fallzeer/Fallzeer\|Fallzeer]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Cynndarr/Cynndarr\|Cynndarr]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Cyllvarr/Cyllvarr\|Cyllvarr]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Korrmear/Korrmear\|Korrmear]]. 

---
#### Culture
Home of the Elven Dynasty, they are so intolerant to the lower class that the lower class is completely nonexistent, as everyone is considered equal enough through force of sheer classism. They are also completely intolerant to other races cultures, ideals and general status.

---
#### Flora/Fauna
Regional fauna are observed to be bigger, more extravagant and there seems to be a lesser population of predatory animals. They also seem more magically inclined, perhaps not through power but because of an enhanced connection to the local natural energies.
This may have culturally caused the taboo of hunting seen in the residents of [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Cauldear\|Cauldear]], but more research is advised.
