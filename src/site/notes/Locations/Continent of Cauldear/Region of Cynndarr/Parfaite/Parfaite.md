A small city in [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Region of Cynndarr/Cynndarr\|Cynndarr]], [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Cauldear\|Cauldear]], similar in build to [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Fort Determination\|Fort Determination]]. It is a bustling compact military city.
The architecture is very connected, with bridges and platforms on each entrance and exit in a web of connection.
The buildings are made of different types of stones with birch wood detailings, and the streets are compacted, paved dirt.
