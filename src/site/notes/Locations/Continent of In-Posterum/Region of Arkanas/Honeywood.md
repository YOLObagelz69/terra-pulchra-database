A logging town in [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arkanas/Arkanas\|Arkanas]], [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum]], caught between the turf of [[NPCs/Groups/The Goblins\|Goblins]] and [[NPCs/Groups/The Orcs\|Orcs]]. Not being able to gain the help of neighboring towns, the town of Honeywood has been in a state of gradual decay. 
[[!Players/The Players\|The Players]] have found Honeywood during a desperate time, and despite tensions arising were allowed in along with [[NPCs/Jelesain the Coachman\|Jelesain the Coachman]]. 
Honeywood is headed by a large norseman named [[NPCs/Fatebound/Bernal\|Bernal]], and was the refuge of [[!Players/John\|John]] and [[!Players/Eleanor\|Eleanor]].
