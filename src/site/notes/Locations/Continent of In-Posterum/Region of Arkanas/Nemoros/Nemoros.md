Capital city of [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arkanas/Arkanas\|Arkanas]], [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum]] and home of [[NPCs/The Emperor of Nemoros\|The Emperor]] and his Archmagis [[NPCs/Azuley Alastair\|Azuley Alastair]].
Due to some magic woven by [[NPCs/Azuley Alastair\|Alastair]], it is impossible to take advantage of a critical roll while within the bounds of the city.
