The remains of a gigantic forest in [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Oalorial/Oalorial\|Oalorial]], [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum]].
Before hunters poached [[Lore/Celestial Beasts\|The Bird]] that lived under the [[Locations/Chain Franchises/Celestial Trees\|Celestial Tree]] that grew here on [[,Time/Timeline 2.0#Augry 22nd, 8309a - Torthul - Waxing Gibbous - Session 1ao\|Augry 21st, 8309a]], the forest was likely a beautiful old growth forest with tons of wildlife; But now it has become a husk of its former self. The tree has lost all of its leaves and its wood has become brittle, rapidly decaying into a thin smokey ash known as the [[Ashes of Dispair\|Ashes of Dispair]]. Within about 30 hours of the [[Lore/Celestial Beasts\|celestial beast]] falling, the heartwood of the tree was already mostly gone, and its hollow boroughs led all the way to the branches several miles up. 
The surrounding forest was also decaying in a wave of death, reaching several miles in the aforementioned 30 or so hours. Whatever wildlife used to reside there, is now no where to be found.
