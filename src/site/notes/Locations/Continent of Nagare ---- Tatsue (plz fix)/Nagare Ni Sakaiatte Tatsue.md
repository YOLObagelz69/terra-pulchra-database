A continent to the South East of Pulchra Terra, home to the biggest city in the world.
Its South East forest contains a [[Locations/Chain Franchises/Celestial Trees\|Celestial Tree]] and its [[Lore/Celestial Beasts\|Celestial Beast]], and is the original homelands of [[!Players/Mháthair Nádúr\|Mháthair Nádúr]].
