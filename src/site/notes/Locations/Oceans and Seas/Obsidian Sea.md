A large stretch of water which spans the gap between the continent of [[Locations/Continent of Cauldear/Cauldear\|Cauldear]] and [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum]].
The Obsidian Sea is named as such because the entire sea floor is glassed with obsidian.
The water's color changes throughout the year, seemingly relating to the Fey in some way.
In the Summer it becomes deep blue, Autumn becomes gold, Spring is white, and in the winter it becomes grey.
