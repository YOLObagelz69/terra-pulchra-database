A gigantic black "dog" with a mechanical harness that provides certain bonuses.
Mariachi also had the experience of having battled [[NPCs/Charlie/Charlie\|Charlie]] in the past.

On his back is a mechanical harness which has a few gadgets and doodads. Upon mounting him, handlebars sprout out and his speed becomes hundreds of feet per second. The harness also has a gun on it, with three differently colored buttons. 
	Red - Combat related, a gun that shoots.
	Green - A stim gun, which theoretically heals
	Purple - A spinning dish, we aren't sure for what, but our best guess is anti-air defense


==A remnant of a dead universe, Mariachi was "collected" by a man named Roman Lee in the distant future.==
