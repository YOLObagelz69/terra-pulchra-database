A sunflower found and raised in the frozen jail cells of [[Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Fort Determination\|Fort Determination]] that clings onto [[!Players/Mháthair Nádúr\|Mháthair Nádúr]]. When she reaches zero hp and would otherwise go down, the flower grows into a huge plant beast that supports her in her need.

On spawn, produces a ton of pollen that smells lovely to allies, and poisons any enemies.
