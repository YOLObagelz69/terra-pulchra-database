Capital city of [[Relay/Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Arrana\|Arrana]], [[Relay/Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/In-Posterum\|In-Posterum]] and home of [[Relay/NPCs/The Emperor of Nemoros\|The Emperor]] and his Archmagis [[Relay/NPCs/Azuley Alastair\|Azuley Alastair]].
Due to some magic woven by [[Relay/NPCs/Azuley Alastair\|Alastair]], it is impossible to take advantage of a critical roll while within the bounds of the city.
