Its South East forest contains a [[Relay/Locations/Chain Franchises/Celestial Trees\|Celestial Tree]] and its [[Relay/Lore/Celestial Beasts\|Celestial Beast]], and is the original homelands of [[Relay/!Players/Mháthair Nádúr\|Mháthair Nádúr]].
