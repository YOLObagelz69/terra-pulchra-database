*"The victims of the Skirmish of Fire wish for your word"*
A high priest located within the [[Relay/Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Nemoros/The Center of the World\|The Center of the World]] 
When high ranking members of the [[Relay/Locations/Continent of In-Posterum/Region of Arrana/Nemoros/Nemoros\|Nemoros]] council die, they call Father Tobias to revive them. Because of this, each councilman holds a 500gp diamond to fuel the spells.
